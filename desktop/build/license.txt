DATA AND ACCOUNT CONNECTION

The desktop client connects to faircroft.online over HTTPS. It does not connect directly to the Arma Reforger server, RCON, SFTP, or game-mod secrets. Closing or uninstalling the desktop client does not delete your Faircroft web account or unlink your Arma account.

UPDATES

The client checks the official Faircroft GitHub release channel for updates. Updates may download in the background. You will be offered the option to restart and install an available update; choosing Later installs it when the application closes.
